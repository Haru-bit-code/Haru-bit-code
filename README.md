## About

I came to AI through physics. My MSc thesis at Mahatma Gandhi University was a Python simulation of two-stream plasma instability, which taught me numerical modelling, statistics and how to debug results that look wrong. I then completed the Data Science & AI certification at the Boston Institute of Analytics (capstone: fraud detection).

Now I build AI systems end to end: the data pipeline, the model, the API, and the interface someone actually uses. I'm currently a **Full-Stack & Backend Developer Intern at Onboard360**, working in NestJS and TypeScript.

## How I work

- **Ground the model.** Retrieval with citations beats a confident guess. Every RAG project here shows its sources.
- **Keep a human in the loop.** Agents that touch files, code or a browser should ask first. AURIX sorts actions into safe, sensitive and dangerous tiers.
- **Ship something usable.** Most projects have a live demo or a one-command launcher, not just a notebook.
